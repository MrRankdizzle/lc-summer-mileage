The Why

Nothing here is "because I said so"

Every piece of how we train has a reason, and most of them have research behind them. The Why is our library of those reasons, written for athletes and parents.

Open the library
Training

Why are easy days so slow?

Because easy running builds the aerobic system without the recovery cost, and it lets the hard days be hard.

 Strength

Why do runners lift?

Strength training cuts overuse injuries substantially and makes every stride cost less energy.

 Recovery

Why does sleep matter so much?

Teen athletes who sleep less than eight hours get hurt far more often. It is the best recovery tool there is.

 Fuel

Why "eat enough" comes first

Under-fueling hurts bones, hormones, and performance together. Fueled is faster.

 Seasons

Why take time off after the season?

Bodies and minds adapt during rest. A real break is part of the plan, not a pause in it.

 Mind

What does "tough" actually mean?

Not gritting your teeth. Real toughness is staying calm and making good choices when it gets hard.
