Who we learn from

The coaches and scientists behind this

Our training is modeled first on Steve Magness, and checked against other people who have spent careers on these questions.

Coach, author, exercise scientist

Steve Magness

Build endurance and speed together, never leave anything behind, move from general to specific, and coach people instead of yelling at them.

Coach and physiologist

Jack Daniels

Every run should have a purpose you can name. Easy running builds resistance to injury and the heart itself.

High school coaching educator

Jay Johnson

Build the aerobic engine, strengthen the chassis, and rev the engine with strides. Keep young runners athletic.

Sport scientist

Stephen Seiler

Documented that the world's best endurance athletes do about four in five sessions easy.

Coach

Arthur Lydiard

The original base builder. Aerobic training is the training that makes all other training possible.

Medical consensus

AAP, IOC, ACSM

Pediatricians and sports medicine bodies on rest, specialization, fueling, sleep, heat and cold for young athletes.
